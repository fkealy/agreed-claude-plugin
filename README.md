# Agreed

Decide together, without the group chat going round in circles.

## Use it

When you're choosing something with other people — where to eat, what to watch, where to go, what to call the baby — ask Claude to help you decide together. Claude suggests the options and puts them into an Agreed room, and you get one link to send to the group. Everyone opens it on their own phone and swipes yes or no on each option, with no app or account needed. When everyone's done, Agreed shows what you all agreed on, and the card in your conversation plays the result.

Share an Agreed link with Claude to add your own picks, or ask "what did they pick?" to check on it.

## Data

The plugin connects Claude to Agreed's server at mcp.getagreed.app/mcp. Claude sends a decision's title, its options and, for restaurants and activities, the area they're in. Agreed sends back the room's links and its progress as counts — never who said yes to what, and never people's names to Claude. Rooms nobody uses are deleted. Full details: https://getagreed.app/privacy
